\section{Conclusion}

\system{} treats inference tuning as evidence allocation: jointly choose a
configuration and the least costly fidelity capable of resolving an SLO/Pareto
decision.  Its bounded planner, cross-fidelity model, \ipig{} policy, and L0--L4
contract connect simulation to measured calibration without presenting either
as cluster truth.  The implemented H100 pilot connects a frozen L0 prediction
to three blind L1 repeats without holdout leakage, yielding \PilotMeanMAPE{}
MAPE across energy, throughput, P95 TTFT, and P95 TPOT.  Its
\PilotEnergyAPE{} energy error shows why one-anchor simulation must request
more evidence.  The H100/H200/B200 campaign must now establish calibrated
workload and topology transfer, Pareto information gain per GPU-hour, agent
value, and independent target-scale verification.
