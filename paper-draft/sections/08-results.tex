\subsection{Prediction and Cross-Scale Fidelity}

The final paper will report measured-versus-predicted parity plots for energy,
TTFT, and TPOT, separated into interpolation, unseen token/batch region, and
unseen node-count splits.  A calibration curve will test whether nominal
intervals achieve \Coverage{} coverage.  The central quantities are energy
MAPE (\EnergyMAPE) and TPOT MAPE (\TPOTMAPE), with error reported before and
after one, two, and four sparse L3 observations.

\subsection{Pareto-Search Efficiency and Energy Savings}

Best-so-far feasible hypervolume and J/token will be plotted against both
measurement count and cumulative GPU-hours.  Curves will compare \system,
random search, single-fidelity multi-objective Bayesian optimization,
multi-fidelity multi-objective Bayesian optimization, and simulator-only search
under identical seeds and budgets.  The primary outcomes are Pareto recall
(\ParetoRecall), normalized hypervolume-regret reduction (\HypervolumeGain),
GPU-hour reduction (\MeasurementReduction), and verified energy savings over
runtime defaults (\EnergySaving) at an SLO pass rate of \SLOPassRate.

\subsection{Case Studies and Ablations}

Three trace-level case studies are reserved: (i) an 8B prefill-heavy case, (ii)
a one-node 70B decode-heavy case where the agent can reject ineffective power
capping, and (iii) a two-node 70B case where communication and placement change
the recommendation.  Ablations separate the Energy Twin, \ipig, semantic
planner, and L4 gate.  The invalid-action rate (\InvalidActionRate) and agent
runtime/token cost (\AgentOverhead) are reported separately from measurement
cost.
