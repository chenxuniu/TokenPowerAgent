\section{Evaluation}

\subsection{Platform and Frozen Protocol}

We test workload transfer on one NVIDIA H100 80GB at a 700-W limit (driver
580.173.02, DCGM 4.6.1).  A digest-pinned vLLM 0.23.0 container serves the
pinned Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct revision in BF16.  The server uses
TP$=$PP$=$DP$=$1, 256 maximum sequences, 8192 maximum batched tokens, chunked
prefill, and disabled prefix caching.  Every workload generates 128 output
tokens at unbounded offered rate; request count is eight times concurrency.

The protocol has four chronological stages.  First, \AnchorRepeats{} runs at
512 input tokens and concurrency 8 form the L1 anchor.  Second,
\DevelopmentWorkloads{} workloads spanning 256--2,048 input tokens and
concurrency 1--32, with three repeats each, fit the residual model; its
leave-one-workload-out energy MAPE is \DevelopmentLOOEnergyMAPE{}.  Third, we
freeze \HoldoutWorkloads{} disjoint context/concurrency predictions before
collecting \HoldoutMeasurements{} cyclically balanced H100 measurements.  The
validator verifies chronology and 62 raw/configuration artifacts.  Finally,
without refitting, we preregister and freeze a second $3\times3$ grid:
$L_{in}\in\{384,768,1536\}$ and $k\in\{1,2,4\}$.  Its
\ConfirmationMeasurements{} runs and 61 artifacts test an all-workload energy
MAPE threshold of 15\% and a TTFT release boundary at $k=4$.

\subsection{Blind Holdout}

Table~\ref{tab:holdout-validation} reports the first holdout.  The correction
lowers energy MAPE from \BaseEnergyMAPE{} to \CalibratedEnergyMAPE{}; median
and maximum energy APE are \EnergyMedianAPE{} and \EnergyMaxAPE{}.  Rank
correlation is $\rho=\EnergySpearman{}$, and \EnergyPairwise{} workload pairs
are correctly ordered.  Throughput and TPOT improve to
\CalibratedThroughputMAPE{} and \CalibratedTPOTMAPE{} MAPE.  TTFT remains the
weakest metric at \CalibratedTTFTMAPE{}.  At the worst sparse point, opposing
power and duration errors leave energy within 13.77\% while TTFT misses by
138.8\%; energy accuracy alone therefore cannot certify the decomposition.

\subsection{No-Refit Scope Confirmation}

The second holdout passes its preregistered primary endpoint: energy MAPE is
\ConfirmationEnergyMAPE{}, maximum APE is \ConfirmationEnergyMaxAPE{},
$\rho=\ConfirmationEnergySpearman{}$, and \ConfirmationEnergyPairwise{} pairs
are correctly ordered.  The predicted and measured minimum-energy workload
also match.  This is replication with the unchanged v2 profile, not a model
selected after viewing the first holdout.

The occupancy rule is equally decisive.  At $k=4$, TTFT MAPE is
\KFourTTFTMAPE{} and no point exceeds \SupportedTTFTMaxAPE{}, passing the 20\%
gate; throughput and TPOT MAPE are only \KFourThroughputMAPE{} and
\KFourTPOTMAPE{}.  Across $k\leq2$, TTFT MAPE is \SparseTTFTMAPE{} and the
maximum APE is 124.36\%, so the preregistered result is to withhold TTFT below
four.  Energy remains below 10\% MAPE in every stratum, but the $k=1$
throughput and TPOT errors (\KOneThroughputMAPE{} and \KOneTPOTMAPE{}) again
warn that this may partly reflect component-error cancellation.

\subsection{Repeatability, Provenance, and Cost}

Median energy repeat CV is \EnergyRepeatCV{} in the first holdout and
\ConfirmationEnergyRepeatCV{} in the confirmation.  All \BlindMeasurements{}
blind runs succeeded; each prediction preceded measurement and every client
output and DCGM trace passed its manifest hash.  Excluding the three anchor
runs, \MeasuredRunsExcludingAnchors{} development/holdout measurements consumed
\TotalCampaignGPUHours{} H100-hours; v3 alone consumed
\ConfirmationGPUHours{} H100-hours.  Frozen intervals contain 31/32 and 35/36
metric--workload medians in the two studies, but were not independently
calibrated, so we make no statistical coverage claim.  The final archive
digests are \texttt{2d741792...12ecafb} and
\texttt{9a4dbfa5...22cd3fd}.
