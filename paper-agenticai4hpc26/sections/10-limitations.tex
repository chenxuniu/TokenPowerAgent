\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}

\textbf{Empirical scope.}  We evaluate one H100, Qwen2.5-7B, vLLM/BF16, fixed
output length, and a bounded sequence/token/chunking grid.  Claims concern
evidence allocation on this target, not distributed serving.

\textbf{Fidelity and telemetry.}  DCGM excludes host/facility energy.  Sandbox
and Probe may miss behavior beyond the frozen scope, and the TTFT inversion
shows that energy fidelity does not imply latency fidelity.  TP/PP, MoE,
cross-hardware, and multi-node claims require new calibration.

\textbf{Uncertainty and policy value.}  Intervals are not cross-hardware
calibrated, and \ipig{} is a proxy rather than exact Pareto information gain.
Its \PolicyEpisodes{} episodes bootstrap \PolicyHardwareRepeats{} repeats per
key, measuring sensitivity rather than deployments; it does not beat
cheapest-first.  Replay also excludes the corpus's
\ConfigurationTotalGPUHours{}-GPU-h collection cost.  The
\WinnerConfirmationPairs{}-pair \WinnerEnergySaving{} effect is consistent but
modest and not evidence of deployment-scale savings.

\textbf{Planner scope.}  One endpoint, prompt, and
\PlannerCallsPerSet{}-call frozen protocol yield \PlannerVtwoRaw{} raw accuracy;
the guard, not the LLM, supplies 100\% admitted accuracy.  This supports a
bounded advisory role, not LLM superiority or transfer.
