\begin{abstract}
LLM inference can dominate the operational energy of deployed AI, while energy
per token changes materially with request shape and serving configuration.
Choosing a serving profile is therefore important, but exhaustively trying each
candidate on a capacity-constrained HPC GPU is expensive.  We present
\system{}, a bounded hybrid agent that decides which configuration to examine,
at which evidence stage, and when a full target-workload run is worth its
GPU-hour cost.  A constrained LLM proposes a typed semantic subgoal; a
deterministic state guard admits or replaces that proposal, and deterministic
tools select actions, enforce SLO and budget constraints, maintain the Pareto
state, and release only verified recommendations.  The underlying
\sandbox{} combines a CPU-resident projector, short target-GPU probes, full
verification, immutable provenance, and explicit abstention.  On one H100 80GB
serving Qwen2.5-7B with vLLM, two preregistered workload-transfer holdouts
totaling \BlindMeasurements{} runs obtain \CalibratedEnergyMAPE{} and
\ConfirmationEnergyMAPE{} energy MAPE.  A separate
\ConfigurationCandidates{}-configuration corpus contains
\ConfigurationMeasuredRuns{} balanced Probe/Verify runs.  The CPU Sandbox
preserves energy rank (Spearman \LzeroEnergySpearman{}) but reverses TTFT rank
(\LzeroTTFTSpearman{}) and misses the verified frontier; short probes recover
that frontier with \LoneParetoRecall{} recall.  Full verification identifies a
configuration using \ParetoEnergyReduction{} less energy with
\ParetoTTFTReduction{} lower TTFT than the expert default.  A subsequent
preregistered \WinnerConfirmationPairs{}-pair follow-up confirms
\WinnerEnergySaving{} lower energy (95\% CI [\WinnerEnergyCILower{},
\WinnerEnergyCIUpper{}]) and \WinnerTTFTReduction{} lower TTFT.  Across
\BudgetTotalEpisodes{} sealed replay episodes, \ipig{} nearly doubles normalized
success AUC over random, although cheapest-first remains slightly stronger in
this small search space.  On a disjoint \PlannerCallsPerSet{}-call planner
holdout, raw subgoal accuracy is \PlannerVtwoRaw{}; the state guard intervenes
on \PlannerVtwoIntervention{} of calls and admits the expected subgoal on all
calls at \PlannerVtwoMedianLatency{}/\PlannerVtwoPninetyfiveLatency{}-ms
median/P95 latency.  Because raw accuracy misses the frozen
\PlannerRawThreshold{} gate, our claim is guarded, evidence-gated autonomy, not
unrestricted LLM reasoning.
\end{abstract}
