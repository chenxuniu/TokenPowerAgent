\section{Artifact and Reproducibility}

The artifact contains versioned scenarios, candidate grids, evidence schemas,
CPU-prediction, replay, and serving executors, the bounded planner/controller, four replay
policies, calibration and validation tools, and \AutomatedTests{} automated
tests.  Each agent event records subgoal, planner source, candidate and fidelity,
expected and realized cost, uncertainty and Pareto state, remaining budget,
evidence kind, metrics, and fallback reason.

Three H100 bundles preserve the workload holdout, scope confirmation, and
configuration corpus.  They contain raw 100-ms telemetry, benchmark output,
frozen predictions, campaign files, manifests, validation reports, decisions,
analysis provenance, and nested SHA-256 lists.  The configuration bundle verifies
\ConfigurationRawArtifacts{} raw entries and includes an
\ConfigurationReplayRows{}-row replay corpus.  Predictions precede measurements,
raw artifacts precede analysis, and the second workload confirmation uses the
same frozen model without refitting.

Replay exposes a sealed row only after a policy requests its candidate--stage
key.  Its oracle is formed from median successful Verify
metrics, never from CPU predictions.  Reports embed scenario and corpus
hashes and retain failed episodes.  Audit detected that the first replay runner
globally coupled all actions to one of three repeat indices; that summary is
retained but excluded.  Commit \texttt{c41d8a6} replaces it with an
action-specific, cross-policy common-random-number bootstrap, and the corrected
report is identified by SHA-256 in the artifact.  Sandbox, Probe, and Verify
retain stable machine-readable identifiers L0, L1, and L4; simulated, measured,
and verified evidence remain distinct labels in every path.
