\section{Artifact and Reproducibility}

The artifact contains versioned scenarios, candidate grids, evidence schemas,
topology and serving executors, the bounded planner/controller, four replay
policies, calibration and validation tools, and 58 automated tests.  Each agent
event records its semantic subgoal, planner source, candidate and fidelity,
expected and realized cost, uncertainty and Pareto state before and after the
observation, remaining budget, evidence kind, metrics, and fallback reason.

The two H100 holdouts are preserved as compressed archives with raw 100-ms
telemetry, benchmark output, prediction and campaign files, manifests,
validation reports, preregistered scope decisions, analysis provenance, and
nested SHA-256 lists.  The repository-level checksum manifest verifies both
archives.  Predictions precede measurements, raw artifacts precede analysis,
and the second confirmation uses the same frozen model without refitting.

Replay uses sealed rows only after the policy requests their
candidate--fidelity key.  Its oracle is formed from median successful L4
metrics, never from simulated predictions.  The report embeds scenario and
corpus hashes and retains failed episodes.  Simulated, extrapolated, measured,
and verified evidence remain distinct machine-readable labels in every path.
